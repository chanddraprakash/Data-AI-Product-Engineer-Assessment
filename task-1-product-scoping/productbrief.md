# Product Name - Performance Assistant

# Product Brief

## Problem
Marketing analysts spend time manually checking Meta Ads, Google Ads, and CRM dashboards to answer:
“How is marketing performing right now?”

This process is slow, inconsistent, and depends heavily on specific team members.

## Goal
Build an internal dashboard that centralizes marketing performance data and gives quick actionable insights.

## Primary Users
- Internal marketing analysts
- Account managers

## Core Value
The tool reduces manual reporting effort and creates consistent insights across clients.
